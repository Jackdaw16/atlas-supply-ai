# Purchasing Policy

Purchase order statuses are Draft, Submitted, Approved, Received, and Cancelled. Only Draft purchase orders are editable, and submission requires at least one item.

A Submitted purchase order can be Approved. An Approved purchase order can be Received. A Received purchase order cannot be Cancelled.

Use existing application queries or MCP tools to check live purchase order records. This policy does not define approval roles, shipment handling, product rules, returns, or lead-times.
